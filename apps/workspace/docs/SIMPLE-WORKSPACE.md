# A simpler daily workspace

Choose **Settings → Your setup** to set up your own account. Simple workspace shows your chosen pages first; **More tools** and **Quick jump** retain access to other permitted pages. **All tools** displays the full menu. Your time clock remains easy to find, and phones still open directly to clock actions. Menu choices never grant access.

Choose Colorful, Calm or Focused, then adjust screen colors, text size and your larger-screen start page. More appearance and menu options contains the existing detailed controls. Preview changes, then **Save preferences**. Changes follow your account across devices. Sign-in, Organization and Connections each have a separate Settings category. Finish or discard a draft before switching categories.

## Schedule

Schedule opens to **Employee shifts**. **Add shift** and **Import schedule** lead the page; Day and Week stay visible. **Filters & view options** contains longer periods, employee/community/job filters and **Use these as my defaults**. Saved defaults open the current period on the next visit; old dates and free-text searches are not retained. A direct link from an employee or job takes precedence. Unavailable saved filters are cleared with an explanation.

**Staffing & hours planning** reveals coverage, recurring rules and hours targets when needed. Individual shift cards keep secondary operations and history in **More actions & history**. Existing conflict checks, permissions, reviewed document imports and audited edits still apply.

## Shared attention rules

Overview → **Choose flag rules**, or Settings → Organization → **Shared flag rules**, lets administrators, owners and developers choose two independent organization-wide rules:

- **Hours over the daily schedule:** compare each employee's total worked time across jobs with all scheduled hours that day.
- **Work outside scheduled times:** compare work with matching job shift windows.

Turn either flag off or choose whole extra minutes from 0 to 1,440. A flag appears only above the threshold. Both start enabled with zero extra minutes to preserve the existing behavior. Each employee is evaluated per day in the organization time zone; a shorter day does not erase a different day's excess.

These rules change highlights, never time cards, pay, schedules, overlaps or authorization. Exact totals remain in details and exports. New saved reviews capture the rules used at the time; older reviews keep their original interpretation. Conflicting edits require loading the current rules. An interrupted save offers **Retry same save**, which safely resolves the original attempt.

## Reports you can read

Payroll → **Set up report & preview** starts with Accountant summary, Job breakdown and Detailed review. Optional controls choose title, columns, ordering, precision, a visual overview and exact audit sheets. Check the preview and **Save my report setup** to reuse the layout on your account. Dates and employee filters stay separate.

**Download Excel** is the recommended human-readable handoff: sized columns, wrapped text, fitted rows, colors, numeric cells and print settings. Table headings repeat on later pages. **CSV · plain data** has readable headings and chosen precision. CSV cannot retain cell widths, colors, wrapping or other spreadsheet formatting; this is a [format limitation documented by Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/save-a-workbook-to-text-format-txt-or-csv).

This release simplifies the shared navigation and the daily Schedule, Overview, Settings and Payroll flows. It does not claim that every academic or accounting page has completed a separate simplicity review. Integrations, organization wage policies and remaining original scope stay tracked in REMAINING-SCOPE.
