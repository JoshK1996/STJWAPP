# Café shopping and receipt photos

Shopping is included automatically with the configured **Cafeteria staff** job. Assign the café job through Employees & jobs or staff import; Shopping follows it. Removing the café job removes Shopping. The assignment editor names the source job and prevents selecting Shopping independently. This is a job assignment, not a new application role or a grant of financial authority.

Staff open **My time clock → Add receipt photo** after a café purchase, with either a password or Quick PIN. Choose a saved photo or use the phone photo picker/camera, check the image and purchase date, and save. Store, exact dollar total and purchase note are optional. Staff can upload on or off the clock and view/download their own past receipts after an assignment is removed. PIN sessions still last five minutes and cannot open team review or receipt management. A time-card detail can optionally link an own receipt to that card's current revision; unlinked off-clock receipts remain in the employee's receipt history.

Reviewers open **Overview → Shopping receipts**, the employee's **Shopping receipts** button, or **Linked shopping receipts** in a time-card detail. Choose employee, date range and active/archived/deleted status. The initial view covers the last 30 days; custom ranges cover up to 367 dates and pages contain 50 records. Linked-card review includes all dates for that card. Open a record for the private full photo, purchase details, JPEG download and action history. Organization report readers can read/download; scoped managers see only their currently assigned communities. Password developers, owners, administrators and scoped managers archive, restore and delete directly, including their own receipts. Finance readers and ordinary employees have no lifecycle controls.

**Archive** keeps the photo and history, removes it from the active view, and continues to use storage. **Delete photo** requires an explicit confirmation and permanently clears the photo, thumbnail, store, total and purchase note in the live database. It retains dated employee/job/community identity, source/photo hashes and immutable action/command/audit evidence. A deleted photo cannot be restored through the app. Disaster-recovery backups follow their separate retention; deleting a live photo does not rewrite existing backups. Download first when a copy is needed. No bulk photo or financial export is implied.

The upload stays in memory until the server confirms it. Closing a draft asks before discarding. A slow or unconfirmed save can be minimized; **Resume receipt** keeps the exact save available while the staff member uses the clock or another page. **Retry same save** reuses its command UUID, so losing an acknowledgement does not create another receipt. Reload/update/sign-out guards protect local drafts and unconfirmed saves. Signing out discards local photo state; a request already received by the server may have saved. Reopen My receipts after signing in. Photos are not kept in browser local storage and automatic offline/background upload is not implemented.

## Storage and access

Photos are authenticated PostgreSQL `bytea`, retained with normal database backup operations. They are never public static files, Railway ephemeral filesystem uploads, or third-party image URLs. Metadata lists do not select image columns. Image responses require a fresh current session and receipt visibility, send `private, no-store`, JPEG content type and `nosniff`, and use UUID filenames. Browser previews use revocable private blob URLs that are cleared on account/scope changes.

The browser normalizes supported camera files to a bounded JPEG before submission. The server independently accepts only still JPEG/PNG/WebP signatures, caps the source at 8 MiB and 50 million pixels, removes metadata such as EXIF/location, auto-orients, and stores a JPEG within 2400 × 4000 and 4 MiB plus a bounded thumbnail. At most two upload requests and one native image decode run at once per web process; excess work returns a retryable busy response. HEIC support depends on whether the browser can decode the selected file; use a JPG/PNG/WebP or take a new photo if it cannot.

The logical photo budget defaults to 1 GiB per organization and counts active and archived photos plus thumbnails. Only reviewed maintenance can change the configured capacity (maximum 10 GiB); logical capacity does not replace database volume monitoring. Deleting releases logical capacity; physical PostgreSQL space reclamation follows its normal vacuum/storage behavior. Administrators see usage in review. The initial rollout checks available database volume before enabling uploads.

## Assignment and transaction boundaries

Migration 053 creates a source/Shopping mapping, storage configuration, receipts, immutable events and command receipts. It does not identify customer organizations or automatically assign production staff. After deploying the reviewed web source, maintenance explicitly calls `provision_cafe_shopping(organization, cafeJob, newShoppingJob, reason)` for the exact reviewed source. The operator-only idempotent function locks the catalog/membership tables, validates the active source and existing community memberships, creates Shopping in the same community, adds existing café members and records an actor-null maintenance audit with its reason. The runtime role cannot invoke setup or insert/change/delete the mapping.

All staff creation, assignment updates, full-account edits and staff imports derive Shopping on the server. Import source evidence remains byte-for-byte faithful to its CSV. Deferred database constraints enforce matching café/Shopping membership and community membership, plus the same community for both jobs. A managed archived Shopping grant stays latent on café members so restoring it works for existing and new members. Archive Shopping before its source; restore its source before Shopping. Active clock segments protect both the Shopping grant and its source from removal. Referenced job communities cannot move.

Image processing runs outside database locks. Receipt writes acquire an organization storage lock before account/domain locks, reload current identity/role/session and membership, validate capacity, then save photo/lifecycle plus event, minimal command result and audit in one transaction. Final session proof gates the commit/result. Management uses expected versions; concurrent stale actions cannot overwrite another result. Exact upload retries can confirm a prior own save after eligibility changes, but cannot create a new photo; management retries still require current scope. Duplicate live source-photo hashes are rejected for the same staff member.

Clock restrictions, schedules, pay rates, exact recorded segments, payroll and accounting remain their existing workflows. Shopping uses the existing clock engine and explicit allowed-job rules. Receipts do not automatically clock a person in, bypass a weekly allowed-job list, post a ledger entry, request reimbursement, approve spending, interpret the image or infer tax treatment. No school/attendance/finance document storage is reused.

Image API decisions follow the primary [Sharp constructor documentation](https://sharp.pixelplumbing.com/api-constructor/) and [output documentation](https://sharp.pixelplumbing.com/api-output/). Jev source-screening receipts are advisory; deterministic tests and source review establish actual behavior. See ADR0019 and STATUS/VALIDATION for completed checks and actual rollout state.
