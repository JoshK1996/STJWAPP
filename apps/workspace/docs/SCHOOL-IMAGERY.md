Production preparation consists of resizing and WebP compression, with no invented objects, people, architecture or generative edits. The crest is resized with transparency preserved. Icon tiles retain the entire visible crest on white, trimming transparent margins only; maskable artwork fits within a centered square of 56% of the canvas, inside the central 80%-diameter safe circle.

Use explicit image dimensions and responsive `srcset`/`sizes` for the school photograph. Do not upscale the chapel source into a full-screen photographic background. Decorative duplicate images should have empty alternate text; meaningful photographs should identify the school entrance or All Saints Chapel window. Keep nearby captions visible and avoid placing essential text over the photographs without a legible surface.
